## Summary
The plant in the photo looks healthy. Healthy rice leaves are an even green colour without spots, streaks or yellow or brown patches.

## How to recognise a healthy plant
- The leaves are evenly green and upright, with no spots or lines.
- The leaf tips and edges are not drying or turning brown.
- The stems and grain heads (if present) are a normal colour with no dark or bleached parts.

## What a farmer can do
- Keep checking the field every week, because early problems are easiest to deal with.
- Follow the Department of Agriculture fertiliser and water recommendations for your variety and season.
- Keep the field and the bunds free of weeds.
- Take a close photo of any leaf that starts to show spots or streaks, and check it again with AgriLens.

## When to get help
- If you see spots, streaks, drying or a patch of poor growth, contact your nearest Department of Agriculture or agrarian services officer.
- One photo of one leaf does not prove that the whole field is healthy, so keep walking through the field.
